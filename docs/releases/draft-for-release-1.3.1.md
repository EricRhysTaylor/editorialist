## Editorialist 1.3.1

Resolving a question no longer warns you about a marker that was never there.

A reviewer can raise a question on its own initiative — you did not mark anything in the scene, it simply had something to ask. Resolving one of those reported that its marker could not be found and told you to delete it by hand. There was nothing to delete. Editorialist now stays quiet when a scene holds no markers at all, and still warns when a scene holds one that does not match, because that one will be asked again on the next export.

Questions you did mark are also matched more reliably, when Obsidian has smart-quoted the marker as you typed it and the reviewer answered with straight quotes.
